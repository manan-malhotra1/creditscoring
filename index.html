Rule Engine Console

Two builds, side by side

The same console organised two different ways. v1 groups configuration into six product tabs. v2 follows the L0 to L6 decision waterfall from the Technodysis rule engine draft. Open either, and switch between them from the badge in the corner.

v1

Six-tab build

Configuration split across four tabs by topic, with rules held separately from the parameters they act on.

  • Fallback scorecard, Score bands, Limits & affordability, Rules
  • Rules grouped into an eligibility and coverage fork, then a scored path
  • Band limits as absolute amounts
  • Around 40 configurable parameters
Open v1
v2

L0 to L6 waterfall

One spine in the order the engine evaluates, matching the vocabulary the credit team already has in writing.

  • Seven layers, L0 routing through L6 portfolio controls
  • Every parameter has exactly one home
  • Band limits as a multiplier of the product maximum, plus tenure and deposit
  • Around 80 parameters, with unset ones counted and blocking publication
  • Assess one customer against the draft and see which layer decided
Open v2

Both builds share the same Global setup: one scoring model, one parameter catalogue, one reason-code catalogue, and the same maker-checker, versioning and audit behaviour. Only the per-product configuration is arranged differently, so a comparison is about structure rather than capability.
